Detailed Description

Tests for the trade history: the neural network's inputs and training, and the Orderbook read-outs (resting orders, prediction, recent error, VWAP).

The network has topology {2, 3, 1}: inputs are the time of day and the traded quantity, the target is the trade price, all divided by OrderDetailHistory::PRICESCALE.
